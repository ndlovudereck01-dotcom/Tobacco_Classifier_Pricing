
1. Cloudflare account and zone
Sign up at cloudflare.com.
Add site → dereckn.online.
Cloudflare shows two nameservers (e.g. ada.ns.cloudflare.com).
2. Hostinger: use Cloudflare nameservers
In Hostinger → domain → Nameservers → Change → Custom DNS → paste Cloudflare’s two nameservers. Wait until Cloudflare shows the zone as Active.
